# 2.9 — Walkthrough, contract freeze, exit review

**Parent:** new (the parent's deliverable + exit criteria, given a slot) ·
**Depends on:** 2.8 · **Unblocks:** Phases 3 and 4, in parallel
**Estimate:** 0.5 d · **Risk:** low, but it is the gate

## Goal

Phase 2's real deliverable is not "modules exist" — it is that the whole product
can be demonstrated without a single line of UI, and that Phases 3 and 4 can
start simultaneously against a contract that will not move under them. This
sub-phase produces the demo, freezes the contract, and walks the exit criteria
one by one.
