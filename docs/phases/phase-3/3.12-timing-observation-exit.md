# 3.12 — Timing, observation and exit review

**Parent:** new (the parent's exit criteria, given a slot) · **Depends on:** all
of Phase 3 · **Unblocks:** Phase 5's pilot
**Estimate:** 0.5 d · **Risk:** low, and the only sub-phase that can tell you the
phase failed

## Goal

Answer the two questions the rest of the phase cannot: **is it fast enough**, and
**do people who have never seen it succeed without instruction?** Then close the
phase honestly.

## Design notes

**The eight-second budget is measured, not felt.** Ten trials each for borrow and
return, on the real hardware, timed from the first trigger pull to the success
screen — including the scanner waking, the network round trip, and the person
reading the screen. A developer who knows exactly where to point the scanner is
not a valid measurement.

**Five real staff, before it is called done.** Not five developers, not five
administrators who helped design it. Nurses or porters who have never seen the
kiosk, given a device and a card and no instruction. What they hesitate over is the
finding; what they say afterwards is secondary.

**Observation is silent.** The observer does not help, does not explain, and writes
down where the person's eyes go and where their hand stops. Helping produces a
pleasant session and no information.

**Findings are triaged, not deferred wholesale.** Anything that stops a person
completing the task is fixed in this phase. Anything cosmetic goes to Phase 5's
UAT list with a note. The distinction is written down at the time, not argued
later.

## Work items

### 3.12.1 Timing runs

> These need the Bluetooth imager, which is not yet available. If it has not
> arrived, the phase exits with this item outstanding and dated — a borrow time
> measured by tapping the attendant keypad is not a measurement of anything the
> product does.

- [ ] 10 × borrow and 10 × return on the real kiosk, timed from first trigger to
      success screen, recorded individually (not just a mean — a p90 of 12 s hiding
      behind a mean of 7 s is the interesting case)
- [ ] Both scan orders covered
- [ ] The first trial of the morning included, with the scanner asleep — that one
      is the honest one
- [ ] Server-side p95 scan latency sampled at the same time and recorded next to
      the wall-clock figures, so a slow trial can be attributed

### 3.12.2 Observation with five staff

- [ ] Five people who have not seen the kiosk, one borrow and one return each
- [ ] Recorded per person: time to first successful scan, hesitations, anything
      they said, anything they did that the design did not anticipate
- [ ] Explicitly probed afterwards: was the sound useful or annoying? Was the
      countdown reassuring or stressful? Did the refusal screen tell them what to
      do next?
- [ ] At least one trial with a deliberately unregistered card, so the refusal path
      is observed on a real person rather than reasoned about

### 3.12.3 The seven scenarios, on the physical kiosk

- [ ] All seven worked scenarios from
      [04](../../04-scanning-and-checkout-flows.md) walked through on the iPad, in
      both scan orders where applicable
- [ ] An unregistered card refused with clear guidance, and the kiosk provably
      unable to create a user (checked against the API, not by reading the code)
- [ ] Camera fallback decodes a device label with the scanner powered off
- [ ] The API killed mid-session shows the offline screen, never a browser error
- [ ] iPad reloaded mid-session resumes correctly
- [ ] Guided Access prevents leaving the app

### 3.12.4 Exit review

- [ ] Every parent exit criterion walked and ticked, or explicitly carried with an
      owner and a phase
- [ ] A short recorded walkthrough (2–3 minutes, the phone is fine) — the artefact
      Phase 5's pilot training and the UAT script are built from
- [ ] Findings triaged: blockers fixed now, cosmetics to the Phase 5 list
- [ ] Runbook signed off by someone who was not involved in writing it
- [ ] `docs/` updated wherever the implementation diverged from
      [07](../../07-kiosk-app.md) — Phase 4 and Phase 6 read those pages as the
      contract

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Borrow < **8 s** and return < **6 s**, over 10 trials each, on real hardware
- [ ] Five staff completed a borrow and a return with no instruction; every
      hesitation recorded and triaged
- [ ] All seven scenarios reproduced on the physical kiosk
- [ ] Every parent exit criterion ticked or explicitly carried
- [ ] Walkthrough recorded and committed to the docs

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The timing target is missed | Measure early — the instrumentation exists from 3.3a — so a slow path is found while there is still time to fix it, not on the last afternoon |
| Observation is skipped because the phase is late | It is the exit criterion, not a nicety: a kiosk nobody can use without instruction has failed regardless of what the tests say |
| Findings become a backlog nobody reads | Triaged the same day into "fix now" and "Phase 5 UAT", each with an owner |
