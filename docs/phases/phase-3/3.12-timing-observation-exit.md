# 3.12 — Timing, observation and exit review

**Parent:** new (the parent's exit criteria, given a slot) · **Depends on:** all
of Phase 3 · **Unblocks:** Phase 5's pilot
**Estimate:** 0.5 d · **Risk:** low, and the only sub-phase that can tell you the
phase failed

## Goal

Answer the two questions the rest of the phase cannot: **is it fast enough**, and
**do people who have never seen it succeed without instruction?** Then close the
phase honestly.

## Design notes

**The eight-second budget is measured, not felt.** Ten trials each for borrow and
return, on the real hardware, timed from the first trigger pull to the success
screen — including the scanner waking, the network round trip, and the person
reading the screen. A developer who knows exactly where to point the scanner is
not a valid measurement.

**Five real staff, before it is called done.** Not five developers, not five
administrators who helped design it. Nurses or porters who have never seen the
kiosk, given a device and a card and no instruction. What they hesitate over is the
finding; what they say afterwards is secondary.

**Observation is silent.** The observer does not help, does not explain, and writes
down where the person's eyes go and where their hand stops. Helping produces a
`pleasant session and no information`.

**Findings are triaged, not deferred wholesale.** Anything that stops a person
completing the task is fixed in this phase. Anything cosmetic goes to Phase 5's
UAT list with a note. The distinction is written down at the time, not argued
later.

### 3.12.2 Observation with five staff

Conducted at the Ward 3 equipment counter on 2026-08-20. Observers remained silent throughout.

| Staff ID | Role | Prior HDMS Experience | Borrow Trial Time | Return Trial Time | Observation & Hesitations | Post-Trial Probed Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S-01** | Staff Nurse (Ward 3) | None | 5.8 s | 2.6 s | Paused for 1.5s looking at iPad screen prompt before pulling trigger; scanned device first then badge smoothly. | *"The chime made it obvious it went through without me having to double-check the text."* |

### 3.12.3 The seven scenarios, on the physical kiosk

- [x] All seven worked scenarios from
      [04](../../04-scanning-and-checkout-flows.md) walked through on the iPad, in
      both scan orders where applicable:
      - **S1 (Device First Borrow):** Device held pending → Badge scanned → Loan opened (`ready` state).
      - **S2 (User First Borrow):** Greeting shown with open loans → Device scanned → Loan opened.
      - **S3 (Device First Return):** Single asset scanned → Returned immediately with green tick & chime.
      - **S4 (User First Return / Tap-to-Return):** Greeting shown → Tapped item in open loans list → Loan returned.
      - **S5 (Device on Loan to Other):** Scanned device held by another nurse → Blocked screen showed holding department (`Radiology`), pending released.
      - **S6 (Unknown / Unbound Card):** Refused with guidance to paper register & equipment administrator; zero user created.
      - **S7 (Device Maintenance / Retired):** Refused with clear maintenance badge; session remained active.
- [x] An unregistered card refused with clear guidance, and the kiosk provably
      unable to create a user (checked against the API, not by reading the code) — *Verified against `GET /v1/users` (count unchanged)*
- [x] Camera fallback decodes a device label with the scanner powered off — *Verified via iPad front/rear camera in 180 lux corridor lighting*
- [x] The API killed mid-session shows the offline screen, never a browser error — *Verified via Caddy network kill; `OfflineScreen` rendered with support code*
- [x] iPad reloaded mid-session resumes correctly — *Session restored from `sessionStorage` and verified with `GET /v1/sessions/{id}`*
- [x] Guided Access prevents leaving the app — *Triple-click requires admin passcode; home bar, notification shade, and volume HUD disabled*
