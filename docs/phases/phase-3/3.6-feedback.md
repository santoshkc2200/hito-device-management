# 3.6 — Feedback

**Parent:** [§3.6](../phase-3-kiosk-app.md) · **Depends on:** 3.3b ·
**Unblocks:** 3.9, 3.12
**Estimate:** 0.5 d · **Risk:** low, high payoff

## Goal

Tell the borrower what happened when they are looking at the device in their
hands, not at the screen. Colour, icon, motion and a short sound per outcome, from
the table in [07](../../07-kiosk-app.md).

## Design notes

**Multi-channel because the screen is not being watched.** At the moment a scan
completes, the borrower is usually looking at the laptop, the trolley, or their
colleague. The sound is what tells them it worked; the screen confirms it when
they look up.

**This is a hospital corridor.** Sounds are short (< 300 ms), quiet, and
distinctly different from each other rather than loud: a rising two-tone for
borrow, falling for return, a low buzz for a rejection. A per-kiosk mute exists
and is respected everywhere, including the error paths.

**Audio must be preloaded and unlocked.** iOS will not play audio until a user
gesture has occurred on the page. The unlock happens on the first interaction
after load (including a scan, which arrives as keystrokes) and the buffers are
decoded at startup — a sound that arrives 400 ms late is worse than no sound.

**Haptics are unavailable on iPadOS.** The Vibration API is not implemented in
Safari. The table degrades gracefully: visual and audio carry the load, and no
code path assumes a vibration happened.

**Motion communicates state, it does not decorate.** Transitions ≤ 200 ms, one
motion per outcome, and `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured by replacing movement
with an instant state change — never by leaving a half-animation.

**The countdown ring appears late, on purpose.** Showing a timer for the whole
45 s makes an unhurried transaction feel like an exam. It appears in the final 8 s,
where it is genuinely informative, and it is driven by `expiresAt`
([3.2](3.2-kiosk-state-machine.md)), not a local countdown.

## Work items

### 3.6.1 Visual and motion

- [ ] One `Feedback` component keyed on `tone` + `outcome.kind`: colour, icon,
      entrance motion, dwell time
- [ ] Icon and word always accompany colour (no meaning by colour alone)
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout, verified by a test that sets
      the media query
- [ ] `CountdownRing` from `expiresAt`, appearing at 8 s remaining, with an
      accessible text equivalent

### 3.6.2 Audio

- [ ] `apps/kiosk/src/lib/audio.ts` — Web Audio, buffers decoded at startup,
      unlocked on first gesture
- [ ] Three sounds: borrow, return, reject; plus a soft click for "scan accepted,
      waiting"
- [ ] Per-kiosk mute in kiosk config, honoured by every call site (one function,
      one check)
- [ ] Playback failure is silent — a browser that refuses audio must not surface
      an error, and must not block the transition
- [ ] Sounds authored or sourced with a licence noted in the repo

### 3.6.3 Wiring

- [ ] Feedback is triggered from the machine's transitions, not from each screen,
      so an outcome cannot be announced twice or announced by a screen that
      re-renders
- [ ] `duplicate` outcomes produce no sound and no motion

## Tests

- [ ] `everyOutcomeKindHasIconWordAndColour` — a table test over the enum, so a new
      kind fails loudly
- [ ] `mutedKioskPlaysNothing`
- [ ] `audioFailureDoesNotThrowOrBlockTransition`
- [ ] `reducedMotionSkipsAnimationButKeepsState`
- [ ] `countdownRingAppearsInFinalEightSeconds` (fake timers)
- [ ] `duplicateProducesNoFeedback`
- [ ] `feedbackFiresOncePerOutcome` — guards against double-announce on re-render

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Each outcome is distinguishable with the screen covered (sound alone) and
      with the sound muted (screen alone)
- [ ] Nothing is conveyed by colour alone, verified for common colour-vision
      deficiencies in [3.9](3.9-visual-design.md)
- [ ] Transitions measured ≤ 200 ms
- [ ] Audio works on the real iPad in standalone mode after a cold start
      (checked in [3.10](3.10-hardware-validation.md))

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| iOS blocks audio until a gesture and the first scan of the day is silent | Unlock on any first interaction, including the scanner's keystrokes; verified on device |
| Sounds are annoying enough that staff mute them permanently | Short, quiet, distinct; the five-staff observation in 3.12 asks about it directly |
| Feedback fires twice on a re-render | Triggered from machine transitions, with a test |
