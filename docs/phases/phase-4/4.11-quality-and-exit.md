# 4.11 — Quality and exit

**Parent:** [§4.11](../phase-4-admin-console.md) · **Depends on:** all above ·
**Unblocks:** Phase 5
**Estimate:** 2.5 d · **Risk:** medium — this is an audit, and an audit that
finds nothing usually means it was not run

## Goal

Prove the console is finished against the parent's exit criteria, with a real
administrator, on realistic data — not "implemented" but *usable by the person it
was built for, without a developer in the room*.

## Design notes

**This sub-phase audits; it does not retrofit.** Accessibility, keyboard
navigation and designed states are properties of
[4.0b](4.0-contract-and-shell.md) and [4.2b](4.2-list-infrastructure.md), built
in from the start. If 4.11a turns into a week of fixes, the earlier sub-phases
were not done — record that honestly rather than absorbing it silently, because
the same shortfall will recur in Phase 6.

**The timed criteria already have owners.** Registration is measured in
[4.4e](4.4-users-and-registration.md) and backfill in
[4.6f](4.6-paper-backfill.md), by the person who built each screen, while there
is still time to fix them. 4.11d confirms them under exit conditions; it is not
the first time a stopwatch appears.

**"Without a developer" is the real criterion.** The exit run is performed by an
administrator, driving their own keyboard, with the developer watching silently
and writing down every moment of hesitation. A developer demonstrating the
console proves nothing about the console.

**Performance is tested on seeded scale, not on a dev database with forty rows.**
5 000 historical loans is the stated budget and the seed is a reusable fixture —
Phase 5's pilot will want it too.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| 4.11 becomes a fortnight of retrofitting accessibility and empty states | Both are properties of 4.0b and 4.2b; a large 4.11a is reported as a process finding, not absorbed |
| The exit run is demonstrated by a developer and proves nothing | A real administrator drives; the developer watches and takes notes |
| The timed criteria are first measured on the last afternoon | 4.4e and 4.6f own them, during their own sub-phases |
| Performance passes on a laptop and fails in the ward | Seeded fixture at scale, plus `EXPLAIN` assertions, not wall-clock alone |
| Deferred items leave the phase undocumented | The handoff lists every remaining stub and every deliberate deferral |
