# 5.8 — Pilot ★

**Parent:** [§5.8](../phase-5-hardening-pilot.md) · **Depends on:** all of 5.1–5.7 ·
**Unblocks:** the go decision
**Estimate:** ~7 developer-days spread across 2 calendar weeks · **Risk:** high —
the only phase whose outcome cannot be engineered

## Goal

Two weeks of real use at one kiosk, measured rather than remembered, ending in a
recorded go or no-go.

## Design notes

**The pilot is calendar time, and it is the last thing in the project.** Every
day 5.1–5.7 slips, the go decision slips by a day. That makes the schedule
question in this phase not "how do we go faster in week two" but "what do we cut
from 5.1–5.7 to start the pilot on time" — and the answer is never the offline
queue, the backups or the runbooks.

**The paper register runs in parallel *and* stays afterwards.** During the pilot
it is kept for scanned transactions too, as a reconciliation baseline: two
independent records that must agree. Afterwards it is demoted from system of
record to overflow lane and stays at the counter permanently, with its daily
backfill routine in the runbook. Nobody is being weaned off paper.

**The numbers come from instrumentation, not from asking people how it went.**
Every exit criterion in the parent is a number, and each needs a source before
day one — which is what 5.8a is for. A criterion measured retrospectively from
memory has not been measured.

**Unregistered-card scans are the pilot's leading indicator.** A high count in
week one means the roster was not pre-registered thoroughly enough. If it does
not trend towards zero by week two, the finding is not "the pilot failed" — it is
"registration needs a push before wider rollout", which is a scheduling decision
rather than a defect.

**Fixes during the pilot are triaged, not reflexive.** Anything that stops the
counter is fixed the same day; everything else is queued and reviewed daily.
Deploying an unrehearsed change at 15:00 on a working day is how a successful
pilot becomes an incident — 5.3e's rollback exists for exactly this window.

## 5.8a Pilot readiness and instrumentation · 1 d

**A go/no-go gate before day one.**

### Work items
- [ ] Measurement plan: one named source per exit criterion, wired and verified
      *before* the pilot starts —
      transaction times (5.5a histogram), rejection rates
      (`hdms_scan_rejections_total`), manual entries, camera fallbacks,
      attendant interventions, **unregistered-card scans**, paper pages entered
      and their lag
- [ ] The Grafana **Pilot** dashboard (5.5b) shows all of them on one screen
- [ ] Readiness checklist: 5.1e passed, restore drill timed, every alert fired
      once, runbooks in place, roster pre-registered, blank card stock at the
      desk, paper pads at the counter
- [ ] The feedback channel exists and the attendants have used it once, in
      training, so day one is not its first use

### Exit criteria
- [ ] Every parent exit criterion has a live data source before day one
- [ ] The readiness checklist is fully ticked or the start date moves

---

## 5.8b Week one — parallel running · 2.5 d across 5 working days

### Work items
- [ ] Live use at one kiosk; paper kept in parallel for every transaction
- [ ] **Daily reconciliation** of paper against system; every discrepancy
      investigated to a cause, not just counted
- [ ] **Daily backfill practice**: the previous day's page typed in and timed
      against the four-minute target
- [ ] Someone present at the counter for the first two mornings — the busiest
      period and the one where hesitation is visible
- [ ] Daily triage: same-day fix if the counter is blocked, otherwise queued

---

## 5.8c Week two — steady state · 2 d across 5 working days

### Work items
- [ ] Reconciliation moves to a spot check unless week one found mismatches
- [ ] Backfill continues daily and stays timed — the routine must survive without
      an audience
- [ ] Work the triaged issue list; each fix deployed by the 5.3e procedure, with
      rollback available
- [ ] Watch the leading indicators: unregistered-card scans trending to near zero,
      attendant interventions falling, camera fallback rate steady rather than
      climbing (a climbing rate means a scanner or a label batch is failing)

---

## 5.8d Pilot report and go/no-go · 1 d

### Work items
- [ ] Report with the real measurements against every criterion, including the
      ones that were missed and by how much
- [ ] Issue list with resolutions, and anything deferred named with an owner
- [ ] Go/no-go review against the success criteria in
      [00](../../00-product-overview.md), with the decision and its reasoning
      recorded — including, if it is a go, the explicit demotion of the register
      to overflow lane and the retention of the daily backfill routine
- [ ] A rollout plan for the remaining kiosks, or a written list of what must be
      true before there is one

### Exit criteria — the parent's, restated as the report's contents
- [ ] **> 95 % of transactions completed without attendant intervention**
- [ ] Unregistered-card scans trend to near zero by week two
- [ ] Paper and system agree on every transaction across the two weeks
- [ ] Every paper page typed in within 48 h, none lost
- [ ] The administrator sustains under four minutes per register page
- [ ] Measured borrow < 8 s and return < 6 s at p95 in real use
- [ ] The administrator states, unprompted, that they can run it unaided
- [ ] Go decision recorded, with the register demoted and the daily backfill task
      kept in the runbook

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Staff quietly keep using paper | They are meant to during the pilot; the measure is whether *scanned* transactions grow week over week, and the kiosk being visibly faster than writing is what settles it |
| Paper slips pile up untyped | Named daily runbook task, 48-hour dashboard warning, timed daily practice, and the "all rows entered" tick on the page |
| People are turned away because nobody registered them | Roster pre-registered in the readiness checklist; the paper lane means nobody leaves empty-handed; unregistered-card scans are tracked daily |
| A late fix breaks the counter mid-pilot | Triage rule, rehearsed rollback, and backward-compatible migrations for the window |
| A fundamental UX problem appears only now | Phase 3 observed real users at [3.12](../phase-3/3.12-timing-observation-exit.md); this is confirmation, not first contact |
| The pilot slips because 5.1–5.7 slipped | The pilot is the last calendar item; the schedule lever is what gets cut earlier, decided in the README's ordering |
