# 6.8 — Analytics

**Parent:** [§6.8](../phase-6-extensibility.md) · **Depends on:** 6.0; after
[6.6](6.6-multi-location.md) if that is planned · **Unblocks:** evidence for
everything else on this roadmap
**Estimate:** 5 d · **Risk:** low technically, high in interpretation

## Goal

Answer the questions that lead to decisions — buy more of these, stop buying
those, this ward loses equipment — from data the system already has.

## Design notes

**Start from the questions, not the charts.** Write down the five questions
someone will actually act on before building anything: how many of X do we need,
which categories are lost most, how long does turnaround take, when is the peak,
which ward is the outlier. A dashboard built without those becomes a page of
plausible graphs nobody opens twice.

**This is the sub-phase that makes the rest of the roadmap honest.** Every other
item here says "measure before building" — [6.4](6.4-reservations.md) says it
loudest — and this is what does the measuring. It is also cheap. That combination
argues for scheduling it earlier than its position in the parent's ranking, once
there are a few months of data to look at.

**Interpretation is where this goes wrong, not computation.** "95% utilisation on
Mondays" may mean buy two more laptops, or it may mean one ward hoards them until
Wednesday. Every headline number needs the breakdown that would falsify the
obvious reading available in one click, or the report will be used to justify a
purchase it does not support.

**Aggregate reporting must not become a per-person surveillance tool.** Loss rates
by department are an operations question; a leaderboard of individuals is a
different thing that a hospital's staff relations do not need. Keep individual
detail on the pages where it is operationally necessary — chasing a specific
overdue device — and aggregate elsewhere. This is a
[09](../../09-security-privacy-ops.md) question as much as a product one.

**A read replica is a "when", not a "now".** Phase 2 measured p95 18.4 ms at 50×
peak ([load-test-results](../phase-2/load-test-results.md)) and Phase 5.6 will have
re-measured on the real host. Reporting will not compete with the kiosk at this
volume for a long time; the trigger is the measurement, not the intuition.

## 6.8a The question set and the metric definitions · 1 d

### Work items
- [ ] Five questions, written down with the person who will act on each
- [ ] Definitions that survive being argued about later: what counts as a loan for
      utilisation, whether disputed loans are included (they are a claim, not a
      custody fact — exclude, consistently with the custody constraint's predicate),
      how a written-off device leaves the denominator
- [ ] One page in `docs/` holding those definitions, referenced by every report,
      so two reports never quietly mean different things by "utilisation"

---

## 6.8b Utilisation, seasonality and procurement evidence · 1.5 d

### Work items
- [ ] Utilisation by category, by location if [6.6](6.6-multi-location.md) shipped,
      over time
- [ ] Day-of-week and time-of-day patterns — the parent's "laptops are at 95% on
      Mondays" is exactly this, and it is the most actionable number here
- [ ] Every headline figure links to the breakdown that could falsify it
- [ ] Queries reviewed with `EXPLAIN` on pilot-scale data, per
      [5.6c](../phase-5/5.6-performance.md)'s habit; report queries are the ones that
      go quadratic unnoticed

---

## 6.8c Loss, damage and turnaround · 1.5 d

### Work items
- [ ] Loss and damage rates by category and department, aggregate only
- [ ] Turnaround distribution — median and the long tail, never the mean alone,
      because the tail is the operational problem
- [ ] Overdue patterns: which categories, which times, which departments
- [ ] Uses [6.5](6.5-maintenance-lifecycle.md)'s condition history if it shipped;
      degrades to loan data if it did not

---

## 6.8d Delivery, export and the replica trigger · 1 d

### Work items
- [ ] Reports where administrators already work — the Phase 4 console — plus CSV
      export for the people who will put it in a spreadsheet regardless
- [ ] A scheduled monthly summary through [6.2](6.2-notifications.md) if it shipped
- [ ] Record the read-replica trigger explicitly: report queries measurably
      affecting kiosk p95 on the Phase 5 dashboards. Until then, one database

### Exit criteria
- [ ] Each of the five questions is answerable in under a minute
- [ ] A procurement decision has been made using one of these reports — the only
      real proof the sub-phase worked
- [ ] No report exposes individual-level loss or damage data
- [ ] Four weeks after ship: the reports are opened by someone other than their author

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Plausible charts nobody acts on | Five named questions with named owners, written before any code |
| A headline number justifies the wrong purchase | The falsifying breakdown is one click away by design |
| Aggregate reporting becomes individual surveillance | Aggregate-only outside the operational pages, reviewed against [09](../../09-security-privacy-ops.md) |
| Report queries slow the counter | `EXPLAIN` review at pilot scale; replica trigger defined as a measurement |
| Two reports mean different things by the same word | One definitions page, referenced everywhere |
